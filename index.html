Value proposition

Documentation ends with the visit. Screening adds zero minutes.

Doctors in the private clinics we interviewed spend 11 minutes of a 15-minute visit writing the note (average across six clinic interviews). Seven Squares 49 listens once and returns two clinical outputs: a visit note with personal patient recommendations, ready for the doctor's sign-off when the consultation ends — and a speech-based cognitive index for every patient over 40, computed from as little as four minutes of patient speech.

Business thesis · secret sauce

One recording. Two jobs medicine still treats separately.

Cognitive screening today is a separate 10–15-minute test (MMSE/MoCA), done rarely and only after a complaint appears. We extract both the visit note and a cognitive index from the same consultation audio — and the audio never leaves the clinic.

On-site processing. Transcription and acoustic feature extraction run on the clinic's own computer. Measured on a Windows PC with 8 GB RAM and no GPU: a 15-minute visit is processed in 70–85 seconds (real-time factor ≈ 0.1), with peak memory use of 0.6 GB. No server, no audio upload.

What we measure: semantic and lexical content, syntactic complexity, bimodal log-normal pause distribution, utterance length, speech rate, and variability of voice breaks that disturb speech rhythm — markers validated in longitudinal Alzheimer's studies.

Evidence. Published models built on these combined linguistic and acoustic features reach 85.45% and 89.6% accuracy in AD classification on the ADReSS benchmark.

The index. A 0–100 score referenced to MMSE/MoCA scores from the training corpora (DementiaBank, ADReSS) and weighted by the model's estimated error — a wellness indicator, not a diagnosis.

How the solution works

From microphone to chart — audio stays on-site.

A microphone in the consulting room records the visit. On the clinic's own computer the recording is split into speaker turns, transcribed, and analysed for acoustic and timing features — pauses, speech rate, voice breaks. The transcript and the computed features, never the audio, go to the cloud, where an LLM drafts the visit note and a classifier trained on DementiaBank and ADReSS (healthy / MCI / dementia) returns a cognitive index from 0 to 100.
